The staircase of abstractions

From relay to register machine, one step at a time — and each arrow opens the sentence that supports it, copied from the book, with chapter.

This map begins almost empty by design. It only admits what someone read: nothing enters by plausibility, by consensus, nor because a model wrote it. 11 edges read and 24 constructions verified, against 10 that are known to exist and have not yet been opened, listed at the end — because a map that hides what is missing lies about its own size.

level 0
relay
A switch that another circuit activates. It is the first component that allows a circuit to control another — and it is from there that everything grows.
is made of electromagnet — construction, and the source says:

Why this way

A computation map generated by a model takes an afternoon and seems complete. The problem appears later: in the twin map of this house, the math-prerequisite-map, the entries were written by a local model and one of them claims, in the air until today, that two sets with the same elements can be different. It's false, and passed because no one read it.

Here the order has been inverted: the human reading gate comes before the content, and the price is this small map. The bet is that eleven edges that can be verified are worth more than sixty that cannot.

The new edges enter through a funnel: a local model proposes candidates, a gate checks the phrase character by character against the book, and what survives only becomes an edge after human judgment. In the first round: 48 proposed, 46 passed through the gate, 34 accepted. The model can find; it cannot assert.
